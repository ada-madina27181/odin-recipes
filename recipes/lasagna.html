Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of savory meat ragù, creamy sauces like béchamel or ricotta, and melted cheeses such as mozzarella and parmesan.

Ingredients

  • Flat pasta sheets
  • A rich meat
  • Tomato sauce
  • A creamy three-cheese filling

Steps

  1. Make a meat tomato sauce
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles
  3. Mix ricotta with parmesan and eggs
  4. Layer the ingredients in a 9x13-inch baking dish starting with sauce, noodles, cheese, and meat
  5. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 35 minutes.
