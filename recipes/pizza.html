Pepperoni Pizza

Description

A pepperoni pizza is a classic, popular pie featuring a yeasted wheat crust, bright red tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and slices of spiced cured meat called pepperoni. In the oven's heat, the smoky, spicy sausage releases savory oils, making the cheese rich and bubbling.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Sliced pepperoni

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 450–500°F
  2. Roll out your pizza dough
  3. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce
  4. Top generously with shredded mozzarella cheese and pepperoni slices
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and bubbly
