Smoked Salmon Sushi Roll

Description

A smoked salmon sushi roll is a delicious fusion-style roll featuring savory, smoky cured salmon, sticky seasoned sushi rice, and crisp nori seaweed

Ingredients

  • Short-grain sushi rice
  • Rice vinegar
  • Nori seawweed sheets
  • Slaced smoked salmon
  • Avocado
  • Crisp cucumber

Steps

  1. Cook and season short-grain sushi rice with rice vinegar, sugar, and salt
  2. Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat
  3. Spread a thin layer of rice
  4. Add smoked salmon and fillings like avocado or cucumber
  5. Roll it up tightly
  6. Slice into pieces
